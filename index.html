About

I’m Charles Yang — a senior at Suzhou High School of Jiangsu Province and the founder of its dessert club. I bake low-sugar, recipe-free desserts, teach them to children, serve them to visiting students and professors at XJTLU, and share recipes in the dessert club group chat.

01 / Philosophy

Low sugar.
No recipe.

Two rules I cook by — and the only two I never break.

Sugar is not the point; delight is. When I cook without a recipe I taste, adjust, and trust my senses — and that is exactly the habit I want to hand to other people.

01 — Low sugar

Less sugar,
not less pleasure.

I rework taste, texture and temperature until the lighter version is the one people reach for twice. Healthier should never taste like an apology.

02 — No recipe

No recipe means
no autopilot.

A recipe is a script someone else already finished. Cooking by feel makes every batch a live conversation between ingredients and instinct — repeatable in spirit, never in numbers.

02 / Dessert Salon for Kids

A salon,
not a class.

Everyone leaves having made something with their own hands.
Weekend workshop · ages 7–12
Why we gather

A salon is a table where nobody is a guest.

Children learn that sweetness can come from their own two hands — and that making it together matters more than making it perfect.

Nothing here is precious. Things spill, cakes crack, and the cracked ones are usually the ones we remember.

40+ kids taught 10+ workshops
03 / Community Bonding

Dessert is a way
to gather.

A small excuse to put people in the same room.

I carry dessert out of the kitchen and into the places people already are — classrooms, festivals, and shared tables. Sweetness travels; it crosses languages, ages, and the awkward first ten minutes of any room.

Club motto

Create sweetness, share joy.
